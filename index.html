Reading plan

How to use this tour.

The tour is ~110 minutes of reading total. You shouldn't binge it. Spread it across week 1 alongside your BD work and ICP research, then return to specific chapters as the exercises in chapter 13 prompt them.

Each row below pairs chapters to read with exercises to attempt. Times are conservative — feel free to go faster if it clicks.

Day 1
TUESDAY · PM
Read: 00 Foundations (skip if web-savvy) · 01 The product · 02 The stack
Goal: Walk away with the castle metaphor + the "browser ⇄ server ⇄ database" picture. That's the gist.
~30 min
Days 2–3
WED–THU
Read: 03 Tenant security · 04 Frontend layers · 05 Request flow (interactive — play the animation)
Do: Exercise 01 (read one repository, ~15 min) · Exercise 02 (see how a URL becomes a folder, ~20 min)
~65 min
Days 4–5
FRI · END OF WK 1
Read: 06 Where things live · 07 The platform · 08 Lifecycle of a feature
Do: Exercise 03 (trace a server action, ~30 min) · Exercise 04 (run dev server, watch a request, ~45 min)
~105 min
Week 2
SELF-PACED
Read: 09 The outside world · 10 Daily tools · 11 Principles
Do: Exercises 05–08 (platform tour, generator demo, skill demo, one senior-eng doc end-to-end)
~3 hr
Weeks 3–5
DEVTOOLS + APPLY
Use as lookup: 12 Reference · 14 Glossary (whenever a term confuses you)
Do: DevTools deep-dive — Exercises 09–12 (Elements · Network · Console · Application). Apply chapter 08's lifecycle to a real ticket if the founder assigns one.
~2 hr
Weeks 6–10
STRETCH GOAL
Build your own product. See chapter 13's stretch-goal roadmap: idea → scaffold the stack → first feature → shareable → show one user.
Re-read whichever chapters apply: 02 (stack), 07 (platform), 08 (lifecycle), 11 (principles). The tour becomes your engineering reference manual.
5 wks

What's optional?

Chapter 00 (skip if you've built websites before). Chapter 11 (principles) is the most abstract — fine to skim now and come back later.

If a chapter feels too dense.

Flag it in your daily log or 1:1. The tour gets better when you tell us where it broke. Written for someone who knows what a function and a webpage are — never seen RLS or server actions.

Stretch goal

Ship your own product by week 10.

Chapter 13 ends with a 5-week roadmap: pick an idea, scaffold the stack, add one feature, deploy, share with one user. Same tools as TAP•IP. Founder mentorship on demand. Your project, your call.

It's optional. It's also probably the single best thing you can do with your summer.
