Chapter 01 · ~3 minutes

The product, in 60 seconds.

What TAP•IP sells, and how three workspaces map to one castle metaphor.

TAP•IP is defensible IP management for the products you ship. We help product companies — startups and mid-market — capture, protect, and commercialize their intellectual property without turning every decision into a lawyer phone call.

Hold this metaphor in your head — it explains the entire product:

💡 → 🏰 → 🌊

The idea sits at the center.
The product is the castle built around it.
The IP is the moat that keeps competitors out.

Three layers · one castle

Customers don't buy patents — they buy products. The product is the prize. IP is what makes the win defensible. Every line of code in TAP•IP serves that story.

The company is pre-seed. Founder-led. Selling to inventors, product leads, IP counsel, and founders at companies that ship things and care about defensibility.

The product split

Three workspaces.

The product splits into three workspaces. Each one is its own area of the app with its own URL prefix, navigation, and features. A customer can subscribe to one, two, or all three. Click any card to see what's inside.

🧪
The Lab
Where ideas are born.

URL pattern

/[tenant]/lab/...

Code lives in

frontend/src/app/[tenantSegment]/(authenticated)/lab/

Top features

  • Inventor notebook + brainstorm sessions
  • Whiteboard with AI sketch transcription
  • AI capture-assist for raw notes → disclosure-ready records
⚙️
The Engine
Where ideas are protected.

URL pattern

/[tenant]/engine/...

Code lives in

frontend/src/app/[tenantSegment]/(authenticated)/engine/

Top features

  • Portfolio dashboard for patents + trademarks
  • Deadline engine across 25 jurisdictions
  • Cost engine with provenance on every dollar

The Engine = the IPMS.

📋
The Desk
Where ideas reach the market.

URL pattern

/[tenant]/desk/...

Code lives in

frontend/src/app/[tenantSegment]/(authenticated)/desk/

Top features

  • Today band — decisions waiting on you
  • Events + launches calendar (with public-disclosure clock)
  • Roadmap, pricing, GTM playbooks, licensing pipeline

Memorize this: Lab = born · Engine = protected · Desk = to market.

You'll hear it in every all-hands.
